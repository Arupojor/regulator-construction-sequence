    Three views share one stage sequence and one tide control: the longitudinal section through a vent, and elevations from the river side and the country side. The elevations show the three-vent arrangement of the standard drawing; the section cuts through one vent. Dimensions are indicative and nothing is to scale. Wing wall layout, launching apron, toe walls and the 2:1 and 3:1 embankment slopes follow BWDB drawing TL-BCWCS-03 (bridge cum water control structure, 3-vent), adapted here to a single vent carrying both a vertical lift gate and a flap gate. Wing walls are shown in elevation beyond the section plane, with their top at EL E4. Chute and baffle blocks run in rows across the vent; the dashed outline behind each block marks the row continuing beyond the section plane. Gate arrangement shown is the common coastal pattern: a hoisted vertical lift gate on the country side and an automatic top-hinged flap gate on the river side, opening away from the structure.
